Founder & CEO

Dr. Sahil Choudhary

B.V.Sc & A.H. — Veterinary Doctor

Dr. Sahil Choudhary founded Vet Paw House to bring together modern veterinary care and genuine love for animals under one roof. With a background in Veterinary Science and Animal Husbandry, he leads the clinic's mission to keep every registered pet healthy, tracked, and cared for — one wagging tail at a time.
